# Unknowns

- Exact public route and responsive state inventory.
- Whether onboarding requires an authorized account or phone number.
- Which messaging provider and supported API, if any, backs the observed handoff.
- Conversation loading, empty, failure, consent, opt-out, and recovery behaviors.
- Asset provenance and licensing.

Unknowns are not permission to infer hidden behavior. Each is resolved by public observation, official provider documentation, or owner confirmation.
